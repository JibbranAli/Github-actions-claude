How readiness is decided

  • The version comes from APP_VERSION (default 1.0.0).
  • The environment comes from ENV_NAME (default production).
  • A build is ready when its version is a final semantic version such as 2.3.1. Pre-release or malformed versions such as 2.3.1-rc1 or latest are blocked.
